# 30-Day Full-Stack Development To-Do App

Open `index.html` in a browser.

Features:
- 30-day plan based on the uploaded Full-Stack Development PDF.
- Three subject filters: Frontend, Backend & Database, Professional Software.
- Click any day to open a detailed task dialog.
- Tick individual topics.
- Mark an entire day complete.
- Progress bar and counters update automatically.
- Progress is saved in browser Local Storage.
- Reset progress button.
- No installation or internet connection required.

The schedule covers the 3 subjects, 12 units and projects listed in the supplied PDF.
